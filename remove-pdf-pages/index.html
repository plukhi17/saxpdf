How to remove PDF pages

Removing pages creates a new document without the selected pages. The original file stays unchanged.

1. Upload a PDF

Page previews are created on your device.

2. Select pages to remove

Click thumbnails or enter ranges, such as 2-4, 8.

3. Download your PDF

The output keeps all unselected pages in order.

Privacy and limitations

PDF contents are processed in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to SAXPDF. The page loads PDF libraries from third-party CDNs, and site hosting or analytics may make separate network requests. This tool removes whole pages; it does not securely redact text or objects within pages. Very large documents may exceed your device's available memory.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove several pages at once?

Yes. Select multiple thumbnails or enter a comma-separated list of page numbers and ranges.

Can I remove every page?

No. The output must contain at least one page, so at least one page must remain unselected.

Does this securely erase page content?

It removes entire pages from the new PDF. It is not a secure redaction tool and does not erase selected text or objects within a retained page.
