How to organize a PDF

Organizing a PDF changes page order and orientation, and can add or remove pages. SAXPDF creates a new PDF from your selections.

1. Choose a PDF

Page previews are rendered in your browser.

2. Arrange the pages

Drag a page, rotate it, delete it, or add pages from another PDF.

3. Download the result

Check the page order and save the newly organized PDF.

Privacy and limitations

PDF processing runs in your browser; document contents are not intentionally uploaded to SAXPDF. This page loads PDF libraries from third-party CDNs, and site hosting or analytics may make separate network requests. Very large documents may exceed your device's available memory. This tool changes page order, rotation and inclusion only; it is not a secure redaction tool.

Frequently asked questions

Can I insert pages from another PDF?

Yes. Add one or more PDFs, then move their pages into the desired position using the thumbnails.

Can I reorder pages on a phone?

Use each page's move-up and move-down buttons; dragging is also available on devices that support it.

Does organizing change my original PDF?

No. The original remains on your device. SAXPDF generates a separate output file.
