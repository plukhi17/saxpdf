How to extract PDF pages

Extracting pages creates a new PDF containing only the pages you choose. Your source document is not modified.

1. Select your PDF

Previews are rendered locally in your browser.

2. Choose pages

Click thumbnails, enter ranges such as 1-3, or select odd or even pages.

3. Save the result

Download a PDF containing the selected pages in document order.

Privacy and limitations

PDF contents are processed in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to SAXPDF. The page loads PDF libraries from third-party CDNs, and site hosting or analytics may make separate network requests. Very large documents may exceed your device's available memory.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. Select page thumbnails individually or enter a comma-separated range such as 1, 3-4, 8.

Can I extract odd or even pages?

Yes. Use the odd-page or even-page selection button, then process the selection.

Will the selected pages stay in their original order?

Yes. The output follows the original document's page order.
