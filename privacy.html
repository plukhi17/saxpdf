Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how SAXPDF ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit saxpdf.com and use its PDF tools. We will update this page if our technology or data practices change.

1. How PDF files are processed

The PDF tools currently provided on SAXPDF are designed to process selected files inside your web browser using JavaScript libraries. The tool code reads the files you select in your browser and creates the output there. The current tool implementation does not intentionally upload your selected document contents to a SAXPDF application server.

This statement applies to the current tools and implementation. If a future feature sends files to a server or another service for processing, we will update the relevant product notices and this policy.

2. Third-party libraries and hosting

The site currently loads PDF-processing libraries from third-party content delivery networks (CDNs). Your browser makes requests to those providers to download the libraries. Hosting, CDN, and security providers may process technical request data such as IP address, request time, browser information, and security logs under their own policies. These library requests do not, by themselves, send your selected PDF file to those providers.

3. Website usage and analytics

If analytics tools such as Google Analytics are enabled, providers may receive information such as pages viewed, interactions with tools, referring page, browser/device information, approximate location, and permitted identifiers or cookie data. We do not intend to send PDF contents, document text, or uploaded filenames to analytics providers. Analytics events and URLs must be configured so they do not contain sensitive document information.

4. Advertising and cookies

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads, subject to your settings, consent choices, regional requirements, and their own policies. Advertising may not be active on every visit. We will keep this section aligned with services actually enabled on the site.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Browser controls may not replace consent choices required by law or by third-party services. Where required, an appropriate consent mechanism must be implemented before relevant analytics or advertising technologies are used.

5. Information we receive

The current PDF tools do not require an account. We do not ask you to submit document contents to us. Hosting, security, analytics, or advertising services may receive technical information when active. Please do not include confidential information in messages to us unless necessary for your request.

6. Retention and security

Because the current PDF tools process files in your browser, the application does not intentionally retain selected source documents on a SAXPDF application server. Files and generated results may remain temporarily in browser memory or your downloads folder. We cannot guarantee the security of your device, browser extensions, network, or third-party services.

7. Children's privacy

SAXPDF is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing of personal information. You can stop using the site, manage browser cookies, and use available controls from analytics or advertising providers. Contact us for privacy-related requests. We may need to verify your request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site, tools, or third-party services change. The effective date above indicates when this version was published.
