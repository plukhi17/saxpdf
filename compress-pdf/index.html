How to compress a PDF

Reduce the size of your PDF online in three simple steps:

01. Upload your PDF file or add multiple PDFs.

02. Choose a quality preset or set a target file size.

03. Compress the files, review the results, and download each PDF.

Choose your compression level

Low Compression uses higher image quality and resolution. Recommended Compression balances readability and file size for most documents. High Compression uses smaller, lower-quality page images. Target-size compression tries progressively smaller image settings, but reaching an exact file size is not always possible.

Because this browser-based tool reconstructs pages as images, the output may not retain selectable/searchable text, hyperlinks, interactive forms, signatures, or document accessibility tags. Keep an original copy when those features matter.

File privacy

PDF contents are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally uploaded to SAXPDF servers. The page loads third-party PDF libraries and the site may use hosting, analytics, or advertising services. See the Privacy Policy for details. Large or image-heavy PDFs may need significant memory and processing time on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I compress PDFs to 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB, or 1 MB?

Select Target file size and choose a preset or enter a custom size. The tool makes a best effort; very large or complex PDFs may not reach the requested size while remaining legible.

Will compression keep the text selectable?

No. To reduce image data consistently in a browser, each page is rebuilt as an image. The output will not preserve selectable/searchable text, links, forms, signatures, or accessibility structure.

Can I compress scanned or image-heavy PDFs?

Yes. Scanned and image-heavy files can benefit most from image compression. The amount saved depends on the original images and the chosen quality setting.

Can I compress a password-protected PDF?

Password-protected or encrypted PDFs are not supported. Remove the password with permission and upload an unprotected copy.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed upload limit in this page, but your browser and device memory determine what can be processed. Very large documents may take longer or fail if the device runs out of memory.
