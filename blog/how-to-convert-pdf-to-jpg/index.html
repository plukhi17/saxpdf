How to convert PDF pages to JPG images

A PDF-to-JPG conversion creates an image for each page, making it easier to embed or share individual visuals.

SAXPDF Editorial · 22 November 2026 · 4 min read

Why convert PDF pages to JPG?

JPG is useful when you need a page inside a presentation, social post, email, or image editor. It is not ideal for a full document because JPG images lose text searchability, hyperlinks, selectable text, and accessible document structure.

How to convert PDF to JPG

  1. Select the PDF. Open the PDF to JPG tool and choose a document from your device.
  2. Run the conversion. The tool renders each PDF page as a separate JPG file.
  3. Download and organize. Save each image, then rename it to match the page or section. Keep the original PDF if you may need selectable text later.

When to use JPG instead of PNG

JPG is often smaller for photographs and complex full-page content, but PNG can preserve sharp lines and text better when lossless image quality matters. For charts, logos, and screenshots, PNG is often the better fallback.

Check the output before sharing

Open the first and last JPG and review small text, charts, and page edges. Large source PDFs may require more device memory, and a page that is difficult to read at full resolution may need a different export approach.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PDF to JPG?

Choose the PDF in a PDF-to-JPG converter, process it, and download the page images.

Is a JPG searchable?

No. A JPG contains pixels, not document text. Use OCR software if you need searchable text.

Can I convert only selected pages?

Extract or split the required pages first, or export the PDF and use only the images you need.
